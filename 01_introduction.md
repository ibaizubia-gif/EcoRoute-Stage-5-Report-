# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Executive Summary
EcoRoute is an AI-based system designed to plan efficient routes for last-mile e-commerce deliveries. Each route is built using four factors: distance, travel time, vehicle capacity and CO₂ emissions. The system covers the full delivery cycle, from receiving orders to recording delivery results, and includes alternative paths for invalid addresses, insufficient capacity, emissions above target and failed deliveries.

## 1.2 Problem Statement
Last-mile delivery routes are often inefficient because of traffic, the geographic spread of customers and the different capacities of delivery vehicles. This increases mileage, operating costs, delivery times and emissions. EcoRoute addresses the problem by automating route planning and choosing the most suitable vehicle for each route.

## 1.3 Scope
- **Sector:** last-mile delivery for e-commerce.
- **Fleet:** bicycle, scooter, motorcycle and van.
- **Out of scope:** long-distance transport and warehouse management.
